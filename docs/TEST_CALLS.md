## Extra calls (not counted in the 15, but each teaches something)

### Background noise
**Do:** Play a podcast or TV at normal volume next to you, then book a checkup (any name and a fake number).
**Expect:** It should still understand you, or re-ask politely. It should not book under a wrong phone number.

### Silent caller
**Do:** Click Talk, hear the greeting, then say nothing for about 40 seconds.
**Expect:** The silence ladder: a soft prompt, a check-in, then a goodbye and hang-up (about 10, 20 and 30 seconds).

### Interruption
**Do:** Ask 'what are your hours and do you take Delta Dental?' and interrupt mid-answer with 'wait, sorry'.
**Expect:** It should stop within about half a second and respond to what you just said, not finish its sentence.
